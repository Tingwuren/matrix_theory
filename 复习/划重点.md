## SVD 分解（奇异值分解）
### 4.4.4 【考点:奇异值分解的计算】
4. 求 $A = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{ll} 1 & 0 \\  0 & 1 \\  1 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack$ 的奇异值分解.

**【知识回顾】**

**奇异值分解(SVD)定义：**
对于 $m \times n$ 矩阵 $A$，其奇异值分解为：
$$A = U\Sigma V^T$$
其中：
- $U \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$ 是正交矩阵（$U^TU = I_m$）
- $V \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ 是正交矩阵（$V^TV = I_n$）
- $\Sigma \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ 是对角矩阵，对角元素 $\sigma_1 \geq \sigma_2 \geq \cdots \geq \sigma_r > 0$ 称为奇异值

**SVD计算步骤：**
1. 计算 $A^TA$ 的特征值 $\lambda_1 \geq \lambda_2 \geq \cdots \geq \lambda_n$ 和单位特征向量，构成 $V$ 的列
2. 奇异值 $\sigma_i = \sqrt{\lambda_i}$，$r = \text{rank}(A)$ 为正奇异值的个数
3. 构造 $V_1 = [v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_r]$（前 $r$ 个特征向量）
4. 计算 $U_1 = AV_1\Sigma_1^{-1}$，其中 $\Sigma_1 = \text{diag}(\sigma_1, \ldots, \sigma_r)$
5. 扩充 $U_1$ 为正交矩阵 $U = [U_1 | U_2]$，其中 $U_2$ 的列向量是 $U_1$ 列空间的正交补空间的标准正交基

**【解题思路】**

本题中 $A$ 是 $3 \times 2$ 矩阵，按照SVD标准算法：
1. 先求 $A^TA$（$2 \times 2$ 矩阵）的特征值和特征向量，得到 $V$ 矩阵
2. 奇异值是特征值的平方根，构造 $\Sigma$ 矩阵
3. 利用 $U_1 = AV_1\Sigma^{-1}$ 计算 $U$ 的前两列
4. 通过正交化求 $U$ 的第三列（使 $U$ 成为 $3 \times 3$ 正交矩阵）
5. 组装得到完整的SVD分解

**【详细解答】**

**步骤1：计算 $A^TA$ 及其特征值和特征向量**

$$
A^TA = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 1 \\  0 & 1 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{ll} 1 & 0 \\  0 & 1 \\  1 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{ll} 2 & 1 \\  1 & 2 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

求特征值：
$$
\det(\lambda I - A^TA) = \det\left\lbrack  \begin{array}{cc} \lambda - 2 & -1 \\  -1 & \lambda - 2 \end{array}\right\rbrack = (\lambda - 2)^2 - 1 = \lambda^2 - 4\lambda + 3 = (\lambda - 3)(\lambda - 1) = 0
$$

得特征值：$\lambda_1 = 3, \lambda_2 = 1$

求特征向量：
- 对 $\lambda_1 = 3$：解 $(A^TA - 3I)v_1 = 0$
  $$
  \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{cc} -1 & 1 \\  1 & -1 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{c} x_1 \\  x_2 \end{array}\right\rbrack = 0 \Rightarrow x_1 = x_2
  $$
  单位化：$v_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left\lbrack  \begin{array}{c} 1 \\  1 \end{array}\right\rbrack$

- 对 $\lambda_2 = 1$：解 $(A^TA - I)v_2 = 0$
  $$
  \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{cc} 1 & 1 \\  1 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{c} x_1 \\  x_2 \end{array}\right\rbrack = 0 \Rightarrow x_1 = -x_2
  $$
  单位化：$v_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left\lbrack  \begin{array}{c} -1 \\  1 \end{array}\right\rbrack$

**步骤2：构造 $V$ 矩阵和 $\Sigma$ 矩阵**

$$
V = [v_1 | v_2] = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack
$$

奇异值：$\sigma_1 = \sqrt{\lambda_1} = \sqrt{3}, \sigma_2 = \sqrt{\lambda_2} = 1$

由于 $\text{rank}(A) = 2$，所以 $V_1 = V$（所有特征向量都对应正特征值）

$$
\Sigma = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \sqrt{3} & 0 \\  0 & 1 \\  0 & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack_{3 \times 2}
$$

**步骤3：计算 $U_1 = AV_1\Sigma_1^{-1}$**

其中 $\Sigma_1 = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \sqrt{3} & 0 \\  0 & 1 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack$，$\Sigma_1^{-1} = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & 0 \\  0 & 1 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack$

先计算 $AV$：
$$
AV = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{ll} 1 & 0 \\  0 & 1 \\  1 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{2}{\sqrt{2}} & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \sqrt{2} & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack
$$

然后：
$$
U_1 = AV\Sigma_1^{-1} = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \sqrt{2} & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} & 0 \\  0 & 1 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{3}} & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack
$$

**步骤4：扩充为正交矩阵 $U$**

需要找到单位向量 $u_3$，使得 $u_3 \perp u_1, u_3 \perp u_2$，其中：
$$
u_1 = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{c} \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} \\  \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} \end{array}\right\rbrack, \quad u_2 = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{c} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

设 $u_3 = (x, y, z)^T$，则：
$$
\begin{cases}
u_3^Tu_1 = \frac{x}{\sqrt{6}} + \frac{y}{\sqrt{6}} + \frac{2z}{\sqrt{6}} = 0 \Rightarrow x + y + 2z = 0 \\
u_3^Tu_2 = -\frac{x}{\sqrt{2}} + \frac{y}{\sqrt{2}} = 0 \Rightarrow x = y \\
x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1
\end{cases}
$$

由前两式：$x = y, 2x + 2z = 0 \Rightarrow z = -x$

代入第三式：$x^2 + x^2 + x^2 = 3x^2 = 1 \Rightarrow x = \pm\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$

取 $x = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$（保证行列式为正），得：
$$
u_3 = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix}  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\   - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack
$$

因此：
$$
U = [u_1 | u_2 | u_3] = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\  \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} & 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack
$$

**步骤5：验证（可选）**

验证 $U$ 是正交矩阵：$U^TU = I_3$ ✓

验证SVD分解：
$$
U\Sigma V^T = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\  \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} & 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \sqrt{3} & 0 \\  0 & 1 \\  0 & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack = A \text{ ✓}
$$

**【答案总结】**

矩阵 $A = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{ll} 1 & 0 \\  0 & 1 \\  1 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack$ 的奇异值分解为：

$$
\boxed{A = U\Sigma V^T}
$$

其中：

$$
\boxed{U = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{6}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \\  \frac{2}{\sqrt{6}} & 0 & \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack, \quad \Sigma = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \sqrt{3} & 0 \\  0 & 1 \\  0 & 0 \end{matrix}\right\rbrack, \quad V = \left\lbrack  \begin{matrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} &  - \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \\  \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} & \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \end{matrix}\right\rbrack}
$$

**奇异值：** $\sigma_1 = \sqrt{3}, \sigma_2 = 1$

**秩：** $\text{rank}(A) = 2$
