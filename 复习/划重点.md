## 正交投影变换
### 6.1.7 【考点:投影矩阵的计算、正交投影】
7. 设 ${\mathrm{R}}^{3}$ 的子空间 $\mathrm{L}$ 由向量 ${\mathrm{e}}_{1} = {\left( 1,0,0\right) }^{\mathrm{T}}$ 张成.

(1)若子空间 $\mathrm{M}$ 由 $\alpha  = {\left( 1,1,0\right) }^{\mathrm{T}}$ 和 $\beta  = {\left( 1,1,1\right) }^{\mathrm{T}}$ 张成,求投影矩阵 ${\mathrm{P}}_{\mathrm{L},\mathrm{M}}$ 和向量 $\mathrm{x} = {\left( 2,3,1\right) }^{\mathrm{T}}$ 沿着 $\mathrm{M}$ 到 $\mathrm{L}$ 的投影;

(2)求正交投影矩阵 ${\mathrm{P}}_{\mathrm{L}}$ 和向量 $\mathrm{x} = {\left( 2,3,1\right) }^{\mathrm{T}}$ 在 $\mathrm{L}$ 上的正交投影.

**【知识回顾】**
- **子空间的直和分解**:若 $\mathbb{R}^n = L \oplus M$,则任意向量 $x$ 可唯一分解为 $x = y + z$,其中 $y \in L, z \in M$
- **一般投影矩阵公式**:$P_{L,M} = (X \mid O)(X \mid Y)^{-1}$
  - $X$ 是 $L$ 的基组成的矩阵,$Y$ 是 $M$ 的基组成的矩阵
- **正交投影矩阵公式**:当 $M = L^\perp$ 时,$P_L = X(X^HX)^{-1}X^H$
  - 对于实矩阵,$P_L = X(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$
- **投影矩阵的性质**:
  - 幂等性:$P^2 = P$
  - 正交投影还满足:$P^H = P$ (Hermite性)

**【题目分析】**
- $L = \text{span}\{e_1\} = \text{span}\{(1,0,0)^T\}$:x轴方向的一维子空间
- $M = \text{span}\{\alpha, \beta\} = \text{span}\{(1,1,0)^T, (1,1,1)^T\}$:二维子空间
- 第(1)问:一般投影(沿 $M$ 到 $L$)
- 第(2)问:正交投影(沿 $L^\perp$ 到 $L$)

**【详细解答】**

**(1) 求一般投影矩阵 $P_{L,M}$ 和投影向量**

**步骤1:构造基矩阵**

子空间 $L$ 的基:
$$
X = e_1 = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

子空间 $M$ 的基:
$$
Y = (\alpha \mid \beta) = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{ll} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

**步骤2:构造联合矩阵**

$$
(X \mid Y) = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

这是一个上三角矩阵,容易求逆。

**步骤3:计算逆矩阵**

对于上三角矩阵,使用回代法或公式:
$$
(X \mid Y)^{-1} = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{rrr} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

验证:
$$
\left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{rrr} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack \quad \checkmark
$$

**步骤4:计算投影矩阵**

利用公式 $P_{L,M} = (X \mid O)(X \mid Y)^{-1}$:

$$
(X \mid O) = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

$$
P_{L,M} = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{rrr} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{rrr} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

**步骤5:计算投影向量**

$$
P_{L,M}x = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{rrr} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 2 \\ 3 \\ 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{r} -1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

**步骤6:验证结果**

验证 $P_{L,M}x \in L$:$(-1,0,0)^T = -1 \cdot (1,0,0)^T \in L$ ✓

验证 $x - P_{L,M}x \in M$:
$$
x - P_{L,M}x = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 2 \\ 3 \\ 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack - \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{r} -1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 3 \\ 3 \\ 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack = 2\alpha + \beta \in M \quad \checkmark
$$

**(2) 求正交投影矩阵 $P_L$ 和正交投影向量**

**步骤1:应用正交投影公式**

对于实向量空间,$P_L = X(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$,其中:
$$
X = e_1 = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

**步骤2:计算 $X^TX$**

$$
X^TX = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack = 1
$$

因此 $(X^TX)^{-1} = 1$

**步骤3:计算正交投影矩阵**

$$
P_L = X \cdot 1 \cdot X^T = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

**步骤4:计算正交投影向量**

$$
P_Lx = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 2 \\ 3 \\ 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 2 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

**步骤5:验证正交性**

验证 $x - P_Lx \perp L$:
$$
x - P_Lx = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 0 \\ 3 \\ 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack
$$

$$
\langle x - P_Lx, e_1 \rangle = \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 0 & 3 & 1 \end{array}\right\rbrack \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack = 0 \quad \checkmark
$$

**【答案总结】**

**(1)**
$$
\boxed{
\begin{align}
P_{L,M} &= \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{rrr} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \\
P_{L,M}x &= \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{r} -1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
\end{align}
}
$$

**(2)**
$$
\boxed{
\begin{align}
P_L &= \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack \\
P_Lx &= \left\lbrack  \begin{array}{l} 2 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack
\end{align}
}
$$

**【对比分析】**

| 投影类型 | 投影矩阵 | 投影向量 | 特点 |
|---------|---------|---------|------|
| 一般投影(沿 $M$ 到 $L$) | $\left\lbrack  \begin{array}{rrr} 1 & -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack$ | $(-1,0,0)^T$ | 依赖于 $M$ 的选择 |
| 正交投影(沿 $L^\perp$ 到 $L$) | $\left\lbrack  \begin{array}{lll} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 \end{array}\right\rbrack$ | $(2,0,0)^T$ | 唯一确定,保持最短距离 |

**【几何意义】**
- 一般投影:将 $x=(2,3,1)^T$ 沿平行于 $M$ 的方向投影到 $L$ 上,得到 $(-1,0,0)^T$
- 正交投影:将 $x=(2,3,1)^T$ 沿垂直于 $L$ 的方向投影到 $L$ 上,得到 $(2,0,0)^T$ (即取 $x$ 的第一个分量)
